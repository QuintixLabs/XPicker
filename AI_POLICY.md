# AI Usage Policy

This policy is inspired by [Ghostty's AI policy](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md).

AI is welcome and can be used to help with development. However, there are a few rules we expect contributors to follow.

## 1. Be Honest About AI Usage

If you use AI, say which tool and model you used, and how much of the work was AI-assisted.

For example:
- "Used OpenCode with Claude Sonnet 4.5 to implement this feature."
- "Used Cursor with GPT-6 to review the code."
- "Used Claude Code with Opus 4.1 to help fix a bug."

You don't have to pretend you did everything yourself. We just want to know what AI helped with.

## 2. Understand What You Submit

You don't need to understand every single line of code, but you should understand most of your changes, know what they do and why they are there.

Don't blindly copy and paste AI-generated code. Make sure it works, follows the project's existing code style and is clean and well organized.

If it looks like you don't understand your own changes, your pull request will be rejected.

## 3. Issues and Discussions

You can use AI to help write issues, bug reports and discussions, but you must check and edit the result before posting it.

Make sure everything is correct and remove unnecessary information. Don't just copy and paste whatever AI gives you.

## 4. No AI-Generated Media

AI-generated images, artwork, videos and audio are not allowed.

AI-generated text and code are fine as long as they follow the other rules.

## 5. No AI Slop

Low-quality, unnecessary or poorly reviewed AI-generated contributions will be rejected.

Using AI doesn't mean you can skip the work or expect maintainers to fix its mistakes.

## 6. Maintainers

These rules are for people contributing to the project. Maintainers are free to use AI tools in their own workflow.

## 7. There Are People Behind These Projects

Issues, discussions and pull requests are read by real people. Low-effort submissions waste their time and make more work for maintainers.

Be respectful of their time and put effort into what you submit. Beginners are welcome, and asking for help is fine.

## 8. AI Is Welcome Here

We are not against AI. It can be a useful tool, and we have no problem with people using it.

The problem is blindly trusting AI, submitting code you don't understand or expecting others to clean up after it.

**Use AI to help you do better work, not to avoid doing the work yourself.**
