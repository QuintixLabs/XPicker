## 📦 Installation

### 1. Download `🏳️‍🌈 XPicker`
Get it from the [Releases Page](https://github.com/QuintixLabs/XPicker/releases/latest).

### 2. Set it up
Run it, and the welcome screen lets you pick your hotkeys before you start.

## ⭐ Features

- 🖥️ Works on **Windows**, **macOS** and **Linux**, and stays low on resources since it's a Tauri app
- ⌨️ Customizable from the start, you pick your own hotkeys and mouse buttons
- 🔍 A zoomed view around your cursor, so you can pick the exact pixel
- ⚡ A pick on launch option if you just want a color fast
- 🎨 Color formats like **HEX**, **RGB**, **RGBA**, **HSL** and **HSLA**
- 🕘 Your recent colors are saved in the app, click one to copy it again
- 🌗 Light, dark, or the same as your system
- *And much more...!*
